STAGE 1 — the architecture test (RNN vs feedforward) and the "what should we
do" answer. Three models, same target, identical splits:

* (a) FEEDFORWARD on the current state only
* (b) feedforward + k-step MARKET DELTAS (prices, inventory drain, opponent
  sell-rate), k in {1, 4, 12}
* (c) SEQUENCE model (GRU) over the full trace

Why (a) may suffice: the board carries its own history. A tile exposes
`planted_day`, `placed_day`, `max_lifespan_step`, `yield_units`,
`pending_care_bonus`, `consecutive_unwatered/unfed`, `fertilized_until_day` —
the consequences of a day-7 decision are visible on day 12 AS STATE. The game
is near-Markovian for FIELD decisions.

Why (b)/(c) may win on MARKET decisions — three things are NOT observable:

1. price/inventory TRENDS (market gives levels, not rates);
2. the opponent's SHED — `private` and per-seat, so we see what they grow but
   never what they hold; their imminent dump must be integrated from market
   drain over time;
3. our own ledger commitments (`relay_due`, `pull_due`).

The v44 agent already hand-codes (1) via `state["prev_inv"]`, which is
evidence the need is real.

DECISION GATE. If (a) ~= (c), ship feedforward and drop the RNN. If (b) or
(c) wins materially, adopt the hybrid: feedforward over the rich state PLUS a
small recurrent channel over the MARKET SERIES ONLY. Precedent: a GRU already
exists in the build chain (`--gru-auto`), currently stale.

If NO model beats chance, the top teams are not learnable from state — stop,
keep the interpretable findings, spend the slots on economy search. This is
the cheap kill-switch and it runs BEFORE any training compute is committed.

## 8. Architecture critique (operator-requested, 2026-09-05)

STRENGTHS. (i) The head aims at the measured majority failure: 44% of losses
are narrow and decided by sell races (close-win %first 0.771 vs 0.611) --
a sell-timing head is the instrument for exactly that. (ii) Learnability was
proven before training compute (kill-switch, 6.7% > 5%). (iii) The socket is
damage-bounded (blend clamp +/-4, pre-_safe_market) and the trainer refuses
to export an uncalibrated head. (iv) Corpus discipline: team holdout,
post-script rows only, bit-exact re-simulation.

CEILINGS, stated honestly:
* The head only touches SELLs. Production is fixed by the tape and every
  layer is quantity-conserving. The head cannot make the farm richer -- it
  does NOTHING for the 29% out-banked losses. Those belong to the ECONOMY
  lever (daily re-screen). Division of labour: economy <-> out-banked,
  head <-> narrow, autopsy backlog <-> own-collapse (27%, unowned until the
  replay ledger frees).
* DISTRIBUTION SHIFT, corrected: our tape holds state near OUR distribution,
  not the experts'. The public input half (market, opponent farm) transfers;
  the private half (our shed/seeds/farm) is off-distribution. The bounded
  blend makes this survivable and also bounds the upside.
* MAE is a proxy. Nine fix hypotheses have died on the discrimination test
  after looking plausible. MAE decides ARCHITECTURE; only hard-band CREDITED
  decides shipping.
* The GRU arm's truncated BPTT starts windows with fresh hidden state, so
  long-range memory is under-measured; deltas k=12 partially covers it. A
  narrow GRU loss is not proof memory is worthless -- only not cheaply worth it.
* Return-conditioning (1.0/0.5/0.0 scalar) helps only if winners sell
  differently GIVEN the same state; unproven, cheap to carry.
