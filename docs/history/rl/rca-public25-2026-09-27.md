## Root causes
RC1 MILK sold on a later clock (184 losses: PRICE 82, ENDGAME 56, SPEND 46). Same units (266 vs 266), 1-4% lower
average price. The rivals place oversized SELL MILK orders (908k ordered units vs our 24k over the same games:
"sell everything in the shed every turn") at hours 13 / 17 / 21, right after collection; we sell on the tape's
clock at hours 0 / 1 / 6 / 9. They are the first milk seller of the day 296 times vs our 206 (1,220 same step).
Worlds with milk shops: BRUNCH|SMOOTHIE, SMOOTHIE|PIZZA, SMOOTHIE|SMOOTHIE, PIZZA|BAKERY.
RC2 TOMATO project not taken (63 losses, ECONOMY). FARMERS|FARMERS, PIZZA|FARMERS, PIZZA|PIZZA: the rival plants a
median 10 tomato tiles from day ~18 (extra land, fertilizer, wheat, a hire): +$8.7k revenue for +$7.9k spend, net
~+$730. We plant no tomato in 52 of the 65 games: our V219 (late tomato investment) does not fire in these worlds.
Opponents: fieldcraft_2887 (11), tetsutani (8), herd_safe_v3 (6).
RC3 WHEAT: more volume and first to market (126 losses: PRICE 96, ECONOMY 30). They sell 36-58 more wheat units and
are the first wheat seller of the day 543 times vs our 107. YARN|YARN, PIZZA|SMOOTHIE, FARMERS|PET_CAFE. The
"one more wheat" family (more_wheat_smarter_sales, one_more_wheat, productive_wheat) plants extra wheat.
RC4 Final-day races (ENDGAME: WOOL 42, MILK 56, STRAWBERRY 20). Lead lost on day 28-29 by $132 median; same units.
Wool (SMOOTHIE|PET_CAFE): they place 1,908 wool orders vs our 1,390 and sell first 132 vs 82 per sale-day;
strawberry: on day 28 they sell first 49 times vs our 19.
RC5 STRAWBERRY mid-game price (74 PRICE): same units and hours, first seller mostly us (117 vs 69); lead lost on
days 17 / 19 / 26 by small margins in PIZZA|BRUNCH, SMOOTHIE|FARMERS. Weakest signal; likely noise-level race.
RC6 FERTILIZER in the opening of YARN|YARN (16, lead lost on day 5): they sell ~19 more fertilizer units early.
