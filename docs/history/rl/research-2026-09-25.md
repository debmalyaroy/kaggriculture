## 2. The two notebooks the operator named

**master-engine-v3 is our base, not a better agent.** v25's `main.py` (the last callable is
`ig_agent`) has the same 186 functions as our `v61.1_bandit.py` less `_rsa_advance` and
`_rsa_future_market`, and all 300 shared constants are equal. So it is the public cha22 chassis,
which is our v61.1 without our RSA layer. Its version history is a rebuild of that meta:
- v1-v7: its own "structured economic policy" (58-240 KB);
- v8 on: the public route chassis ("13 route tapes, R36/R37/R44, V231/V233/V234, room guard").

The "Proven 95.1% win rate" banner is the same static text from v8 through v25, not a measurement.

**meta-atlas-v2 has three different agents across its versions:**
- **v1: its own research stack.**
  - A sparse closed-loop planner on top of an open-loop farm backbone.
  - Price-impact reordering of sell slots, with an optional demand-recovery term.
  - A one-tick WHEAT market maker: buy just before the known town demand, sell next tick. It keeps
    a reserve for route investment and feed. We already have this as R42, the "wheat wash".
  - Route gates that switch only at a shared prefix (`R_a(t) = R_b(t)` for all `t < tau`).
  - A wool route latched at turn 216 from the rival's public cow and sheep counts.
  - A 6-turn sell-timing response during a long near-clone streak, driven by conservative
    evidence of rival supply (`e_i = dI_i - u_i`).
  - Its own notes say route switching and quantity MPC were dropped because seed-grouped
    holdouts did not improve wins.
- **v2: the current top players' own streams.**
  - It replays six recorded episodes of one top team (NIklitaCheporev, two submissions) whose
    719-step physical programme is identical, or the medoid episode of another (Kenjo).
  - Visible-state guards: hand alignment, weed repair, shed and cash projection, sequential
    funding, contested-sale ordering.
  - Two structural add-ons:
    - *conserved livestock substitution*: relabel a registered COW/SHEEP purchase bundle by shop
      pressure, where `P_wool = 2 N_yarn + p_wool/200` and `P_dairy = N_pizza + N_icecream +
      N_smoothie + p_milk/160`, keeping the same pasture, count and service route;
    - *latent-pasture activation*: one extra animal plus a hand, delivered into an already
      serviced empty pasture at step 313.
- **v3 and v4: a two-route "three-day shop router"** converted from C++.
  - The routes differ only on turns 360-431.
  - The switch uses the first shop, fertilizer inventory, and the rival's planted tiles.
  - A 72-turn affordability guard sells surplus in price order.

## 3. The two GitHub repos

**kagsym** is a neurosymbolic agent:
- A network sets a macro vector and a 10x10 micro map once per day.
- A symbolic executor with Hungarian unit assignment plays the day.
- CEM search sets the macro offsets.

Its own results and findings:
- Measured result: **0.064 mean win rate, 5 of 74 public agents beaten** (it loses by $60-80k).
- Why its PPO failed, all measured:
  - the macro head's learning rate was tied to the micro head's through one KL controller;
  - a joint ratio over about 450 dimensions saturated the clip;
  - 11 episodes per update gave the 67-dim macro 11 samples.
- Useful findings:
  - the network's output depended on the torch thread count (the same seed gave $42,242 on 12
    threads and $48,387 on 1);
  - 18 of its 67 dials were dead;
  - it proposes **expert iteration**: an exact rollout oracle at each day boundary (K >= 32
    candidates, gain-weighted) distilled into the network, with PPO on top and CEM outside.

**kaggicultureRL** is infrastructure only: PPO with a masked multi-slot action decoder, a Rust
batch engine, and replay mimicry. No trained result, and its own docs say so.

## 4. Public RL evidence (Code tab)

| notebook | method | result |
|---|---|---|
| hesoponyo, pure RL | entity-token Transformer, BC on 4,600 replays + PPO self-play, Rust env | $81k vs public top-1 (tapes bank $107-160k); bigger nets break the 1 s turn limit |
| zhincez, "ten PPO runs" | PPO from an anchor | 0 of 10 beat the anchor. Four traps below |
| zhincez, "market half" | swap farm and market channels | a tape's market orders on another farm earn **$0** (bankrupt); the pair is worth $108k together. 76% turn-match BC earns $690, 34% earns $3,061 |
| andrewsokolovsky, V4 | BC warm start, then **counterfactual oracle labels** (branch the world at a checkpoint into 5 continuations, score by final W/L/T), DAgger, recovery imitation, paired holdout | promotion only if it beats V21 on holdout |
| rafifariqrabbani | greedy optimiser + 8 small RL controllers, each owns one dial, trained and frozen one at a time | per-stage attribution |
| destbreso, island GA | an owned base schedule found by search | relaxed roof about $229k vs about $73k for the consensus route, both against an idle opponent. Gains alone and gains "in company" are different axes (+13.2k alone, -13.9k accompanied). Fitness: Phi(margin/sigma) |

zhincez's four PPO traps:
1. **Flat reward** from a random start: every rollout goes bankrupt. Fix: anchor-initialise, and
   use a graded reward.
2. **The entropy bonus ate the anchor**: mass on the neutral action fell from 91.6% to 46.5%.
   Fix: an adaptive KL leash to the anchor.
3. **Critic warm-up on a shared trunk moved the policy** (KL 3.26 while "frozen"). Fix: read the
   value from `trunk.detach()`.
4. **A fake win**: the baseline was a mis-indexed "neutral" action.

**What this says about our design:** the farm and market channels can't be separated, and per-turn
cloning and end-to-end RL are both far below tapes. The design that survives is the one we have: a
fixed structural tape with per-turn guards, and a learned daily choice among market-side profiles.
