# The 2800 plan — why the top 10 win, and what both seats must do (2026-09-03)

Evidence: 23,621 seat-records on engine 1.32.7 in `data/routes/index.json`
(cohorts by today's leaderboard, 7,412 teams), per-turn traces in
`data/turntrace` (top-10 300 games, ours 289, ranks 200-400 300), and the
live ledger of the re-shipped v42 pair via the episode service.
Scratch: `.local/lb/`, session scratchpad `traj*.py`, `analyze*.py`.

## 1. What the data says

**The whole field runs ONE economy, top-10 included.** Median action
skeleton per game, every cohort from rank 1 to rank 400 and us: 2 land,
~9 cows, ~5 sheep, 187 wheat seeds, ~40 strawberry, 12 melon, ~290 hires,
17 pastures, 243 plants. Median bank by day is IDENTICAL for top-10, us and
mid-field (day 29: 88.6k / 87.3k / 89.4k). The top-10 finish under 90k in
50% of their games, just like everyone else. Gross sales: top-10 130k, ours
127.5k.

**Bank is a shared-world quantity.** Own bank correlates 0.73-0.80 with the
opponent's bank; P(opp < 75k | own < 75k) = 0.83-0.89. "Collapse worlds"
are worlds, not farm failures: MILK and STRAWBERRY prices are lower from
day 3 and crash by day 15-20 for BOTH players. No farm-structure field
separates our collapse games from our healthy ones.

**What separates 2900 from 1900 is the pairwise edge inside the shared
world, concentrated in low-price worlds:**

| own bank bin | ranks 1-10 win% | ranks 200-400 win% |
|---|---|---|
| < 90k | 61.5% | 48.0% |
| 90-110k | 67-69% | 60% |
| >= 120k | 73% | 76% |

Top-10 overall 65.7% vs our 52.1% against the same opponent-bank
distribution. Their losses are more often "out-banked at >= 90k" (44%)
than own collapses (31%); ours are 38% collapses. Within the top 100,
rating is uncorrelated with median bank (Spearman 0.18): rating at the top
is pairwise edge + burst path + freshness, not depth.

**Our self-inflicted leaks vs the top-10 (same skeleton):**

| leak | top-10 | ours | cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| feed wheat bought | 183 | 414 | feed+fert spend 25.9k vs 9.2k |
| fertilizer bought (relay/pull advance) | 62 | 248 | churn, resold at ~45/unit |
| net (sales - spend) | 86.4k | 83.8k | -2.6k/game median |
| WOOL revenue | 15.0k | 9.7k | 1 fewer sheep, 14 vs 17 pastures, FEED/CARE 274/288 vs 326/333 |
| starving animals day 10 (median) | 0 | 2 | feed logistics |
| hand PASS turns | 513 | 926 | ~400 idle unit-turns; labour is the binding constraint |
| unplanted seed held d15-d24 | ~3 | 20-35 | dead cash |
| milk held in shed d8-d12 | 0 | 12 | sold after the price falls |
| 2nd quadrant unlock | day 5 | later | fewer crops d5-d10 |
| planted tiles day 28 | 27 | 30 | crops that never sell |
| shed stock carried into day 29 | 24 | 6 | they sell 3x more on the last day |

The winner-loser gap in this meta is ~4.5k median. Our leaks sum to more
than that. That is the whole gap to the top-10 economy.

**CORRECTION (12:05 IST):** the "ours" cohort above is dominated by the
v36-v41 builds (Aug 30 - Sep 1, 205 of 292 games). The LIVE v42 builds
(Sep 2+, 50 games) carry the top-10 skeleton exactly: feed wheat 142,
fert bought 62, PASS 521, FEED 326, 17 pastures, 9 cows, 5 sheep. So the
leak list is already closed in v42 — and v42 still stalls at 1919. Also:
the v42 action counts are IDENTICAL in every game (a rigid tape; the
chassis only edits market orders), i.e. the agent does the same thing in
every world. For v42 the remaining levers are exactly tiers 2-3
(low-price-regime play, base freshness, burst discipline). The leak audit
stays as a regression guard for every future build (it caught v36-v41).

**Live pair today (09:40 IST):** v42.0_bandit re-ship 83-48, last 20 8-12,
1919 flat; v42.1_trackp re-ship 98-22, last 20 16-4, 1933 climbing. The
same bandit file converged 2554 on its first ship (17-3 in games 11-30);
the re-ship went 13-7 there and stalled with K cold. Win rate is flat
across opponent rating (64% vs a 2500 field, 60% vs an 1890 field).

**Why an open-loop tape scores 2700+ and ours does not:** their tape IS
the same economy as ours and banks the same. They win the pairwise
comparison in low-price worlds (branching on the shop-unlock order at
steps 88/120/153/160/216, selling into the shops that are actually open,
not churning feed), they have no leaks, and they ride a hot-K burst. We
copy their world-specific schedule, add layers that buy and resell
product, idle 400 unit-turns, and starve animals at day 10.

## 2. Target bands (operator, durable)

Win ALL vs < 2000, 90% vs 2000-2500, 70% vs 2500+. Translated: never lose
the pairwise comparison in a high-price world; win >= 60% of low-price
worlds (top-10 level is 61.5%); no self-inflicted leak anywhere.

## 3. Plan

### Tier 0 — instruments (Sep 3-4)
- Regime-stratified panels: every panel cell tagged high/low-price world
  (day-3 MILK/STRAWBERRY price + shop-unlock sequence). Report win% per
  regime, plus the three rating-band panels (sub-2000 settled teams,
  2000-2500, top-100). Bars: sub-2000 60-0; 2000-2500 >= 0.90 on 100+
  cells; top-100 >= 0.70 on 150+ cells; low-price-regime win% >= 0.60.
- Leak audit as a standing test: per game, feed/fert spend, idle hand
  turns, starving-animal days, held milk, unplanted seed, day-28 plantings,
  compared against the top-10 medians above. A build that regresses any
  of them fails.
- Live readout per band and per regime from the episode service at 30/60/
  100 games after every ship.

### Tier 1 — remove the leaks (both seats, Sep 4-7)
Bandit chassis (src/kaggriculture/agentbuild/v22_agent.py):
1. Feed/fert churn: cap BUY_PRODUCT to herd need; audit relay/pull/plead
   advance buys on the top-100 panel with churn cost accounted, per layer.
   Any layer that is not sign-test positive with spend counted is off.
2. Starving animals day 10: feed schedule from the herd, never from the
   tape's assumption; feed wheat grown, not bought late.
3. Idle hands: fill PASS turns with water/feed/care/collect from the live
   board (the top-10 do +52 FEED, +45 CARE, +41 COLLECT per game).
4. Sell milk on production; buy seed only for the tiles that will plant it;
   stop planting past the maturity horizon; carry stock into day 29 and
   sell it (gate it: sell-timing layers have lost before).
Trackp planner (src/kaggriculture/trackp/build_econ_agent.py):
5. Impose the field skeleton as constraints, not a search space: 2 land
   (2nd quadrant day 5), cows to 9 / sheep to 5 on cashflow, 187 wheat,
   40 strawberry (fertilised), 12 melon, hires to ~12 by day 15, 17
   pastures. The GA searches only within it. Bar: median bank >= 85k in
   mirror and vs reactive opponents, zero leaks by the audit.
Exit: tier-1 bars on both seats.

### Tier 2 — win the low-price worlds (Sep 7-14)
6. Regime study: twins (same opening, opposite verdicts) and action diffs
   between top-10 winners and losers in the SAME low-price regime. Output:
   what they plant/sell/skip when milk and strawberry are glutted and which
   shops are open.
7. Bandit: shop-branch table keyed on the unlock order at steps
   88/120/153/160/216, candidates from the cross-team top-50 pool (2,183
   unused wins), selected by staged played games vs reactive opponents.
8. Trackp: closed-loop regime policy. Tile and herd allocation and sell
   floors chosen each dawn from observed prices, market inventory and open
   shops (wheat/egg are dump-proof, wool/strawberry/milk saturate). This
   is the one thing a closed loop can do that a tape cannot.
Exit: low-price-regime win% >= 0.60 and 2000-2500 panel >= 0.90.

### Tier 3 — the top-100 edge and freshness (Sep 14-20)
9. Fresh meta each build (Kaileh changed strategy Aug 31; the wool family
   gains +14k by day 24): re-cluster the elite pool per build and check
   the current cluster's strength before using it.
10. Endgame pairwise: opponent-relative sell scheduling from observed
    dumps (47/47 losses had collisions), measured, not assumed.
11. Ship discipline. **CORRECTED 2026-09-03 by competitor intel
    (docs/competitor-intel-2026-09-03.md) — the original rule here was
    dangerous and is withdrawn.** It said "a burst below 75% = re-roll next
    day". That is precisely the policy that destroyed the strongest agent
    measured in this competition: discussion 739179 documents a 40-0 agent
    at 2977 and climbing that vanished because its OWN team's next
    submission retired it (only the latest 2 stay active). A re-roll cannot
    be aimed — it evicts your own best.
    The rule is now: **re-submit only to replace a MEASURABLY weaker agent,
    never to re-roll path luck.** A live agent that is climbing is left
    alone. Set a hard FREEZE DATE after which the pair is final regardless
    of live numbers; the post-lock Bradley-Terry ignores path entirely, so
    a bad burst costs seeding only, while an eviction costs the agent.
Exit: top-100 panel >= 0.70, live >= 65% vs the top-100 field.

### Calendar
Sep 10 checkpoint (tier 1 passed both seats, tier-2 candidate gated);
Sep 17 checkpoint (tier 3 on panels, live readouts); Sep 24 go/no-go on
the final pair; Sep 26-27 final ships, converged before the Sep 30 lock.
Contingency: two bandit-lane agents if trackp misses tier 3.

## 4. What a top-10 finish requires, honestly
Within the top 100, rating does not track economy depth; it tracks a
~65% pairwise edge plus burst path plus freshness. Both seats need
>= 65% against the top-100 field in shared worlds, zero leaks, and a
timed ship. The final Bradley-Terry on post-lock games removes the path
luck, so the true pairwise edge is the only thing that survives to the
final board.

# TRACK P BASE-ECONOMY RESULT (2026-09-03, late)

Report: `docs/history/trackp-base-economy-2026-09-03.md`. This closes item 1 of the
integration report's next-work list ("replace the base economy, not the search"),
which was the stated prerequisite before ANY further search tuning.

**The number: the budget-0 path went 44,205 -> 63,943 median own bank on the
integration report's own 40 official-engine cells (+45%). The 80,000 bar is NOT
met — the bootstrap 95% CI is [57,040 · 72,336]. Pooled over 80 cells it is
70,208 [63,771 · 76,200]. Per the mission's instruction the number is reported
and the tuning stopped rather than continuing toward the bar.**

**Wins: 0-0-40 before, 0-0-40 after, zero discordant pairs, p = 1.0000.**

**A reserved third seed set says the headline flatters it.** Paired per-cell
own-bank delta: report seeds 3-6 +14,058 (30/40), dev seeds 11-14 +15,572
(30/40), **held-out seeds 21-24 −434 (24/40)**. Over all 120 cells the gain is
+9,732 mean, positive on 84/120 (sign test p = 1.4e-05), pooled median 54,286 ->
64,332, CI [60,799 · 68,641]. Real, but ~+10k per cell — which makes the miss on
80,000 larger, not smaller. **0 wins in 120 cells, before and after.**

What was actually wrong, and it was not what anyone assumed: **LAND.** Dawn money
sat at $132-$2,600 through day 13, so the cash-gated `BUY_LAND` orders scheduled
for days 4 and 11 actually cleared on days 9 and 14. The farm ran on NW's ~21
usable tiles for a third of the season while ten hands, hired from day 4, PASSed
150-180 unit-turns a day at `fib_sum(10) = 143`/day. The idle crew was eating the
land money and the missing land was why the crew was idle. Two genome values fix
it (`land: {"NE": 0, "SW": 5}` + the mined top-30 hire ramp, which is LEANER on
days 1-7). Each alone is worth ~+3k; together +19.6k.

Three findings that change how this plan should be read:

1. **The elite economy is expressible in the genome but does NOT transfer.** The
   current top 30 (2806-2978) run one coherent tape family — land d6+d11, 12
   melon on day 0, 38 strawberry d5-d11, 9 cows by d8, crew to 12. Every piece
   transplanted alone made us worse; the FULL elite economy scored 46,582 against
   a 58,426 baseline. Their numbers are only good together, inside their
   executor. **The ceiling is the executor, not the parameter values — mining
   harder will not help.** This is a direct answer to tier-1 item 5 ("impose the
   field skeleton as constraints"): imposing it verbatim is measurably negative.

2. **Growing our economy grows the OPPONENT's too.** Paired per cell, our bank
   gains +14,058 and theirs +13,332; the opponent's median bank is higher in all
   five per-opponent rows. Buying more drains market inventory, which raises
   prices, which is worth most to whoever sells most — them. Share moved only
   31.4% -> 33.6% against `v42.1_trackp`'s 43.0%. **The market-share diagnosis of
   the integration report survives the base-economy fix intact.**

3. **This is the third large economic gain in this lane that buys nothing** (the
   search's +37%, joint liquidation, and now +45% of base economy). Section 1's
   "the whole field banks the same" is being confirmed from the inside: the level
   is not the lever, and no further work on our own bank should be expected to
   pay.

**Consequence for the Sep 24 go/no-go.** The base-economy explanation is now off
the table, measured. Track P is not competitive and no gate moved. The
contingency named in this plan — two bandit-lane agents from different base
families — remains the leading option. Anything further in this lane must be
aimed at the OPPONENT's bank, not ours.
